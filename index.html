Thirty quiet days on Instagram

A one-month content calendar for The Quiet Cup, a cozy café for students and remote workers. The month builds awareness first, then launches a new Signature Cold Brew.

  • Platform Instagram
  • Posts 17, four a week
  • Goals brand awareness, cold brew launch

How the month unfolds

  1. Welcome to The Quiet CupDays 1–7. Introduce the space, the vibe and the people.
  2. Your study and work spotDays 8–14. Wi-Fi, quiet corners and focus tips that make the café useful.
  3. Something new is brewingDays 15–21. A teaser, the brewing process, then the cold brew launch.
  4. Sip, share, stayDays 22–30. Offers, a photo contest and reposts of customer moments.

Content mix

    Promotional posts stay under a quarter of the feed and sit mostly in the last two weeks, after the audience already knows the café.
