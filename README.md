# 30-Day Instagram Content Calendar — The Quiet Cup

**Brand:** The Quiet Cup (cozy, aesthetic café)  
**Prepared by:** Sadhanala Nischay Prasad  

A complete 30-day Instagram content plan designed to grow brand awareness and launch the Signature Cold Brew.

---

## Overview

This project delivers a structured social media content calendar for **The Quiet Cup**, a calm café aimed at college students and remote workers. The goal was to practice content planning and consistent posting rather than high-volume output.

**Deliverables**
- 30-day content calendar
- Content topics & formats
- Publishing schedule
- Content objectives
- Engagement strategy

**Tools used**
- Google Sheets — calendar
- Canva — post design
- Instagram / LinkedIn — publishing & sharing

| Item          | Details                                                                 |
|---------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Brand**     | The Quiet Cup — a cozy, aesthetic café                                  |
| **Audience**  | College students and remote workers looking for a calm place to study/work |
| **Platform**  | Instagram                                                               |
| **Brand voice** | Warm, calm, and helpful; never pushy                                  |

---

## Content Objectives

1. **Grow followers & brand awareness**  
   Introduce the café and position it as the ideal study/work spot.

2. **Launch the Signature Cold Brew**  
   Build curiosity → launch the drink → convert interest into café visits.

---

## Publishing Schedule

Posts **4 times a week** (17 posts over 30 days). Day 1 is a Monday. Consistency over volume.

| Day       | Time     | Reason                                      |
|-----------|----------|---------------------------------------------|
| Monday    | 7:30 PM  | Audience plans the week & looks for study spots |
| Wednesday | 7:30 PM  | Midweek evening scroll                      |
| Friday    | 6:00 PM  | Weekend plans                               |
| Saturday  | 11:00 AM | Relaxed morning, café-going mood            |

---

## Weekly Themes

| Week          | Theme                      | Purpose                                              |
|---------------|----------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| 1 (Days 1–7)  | Welcome to The Quiet Cup   | Introduce the space, vibe, and people                |
| 2 (Days 8–14) | Your study & work spot     | Wi-Fi, quiet corners, focus tips — why the café is useful |
| 3 (Days 15–21)| Something new is brewing   | Teaser → brewing process → cold brew launch          |
| 4 (Days 22–30)| Sip, share, stay           | Offers, photo contest, reposts, and month recap      |

---

## Content Mix

Promotional content is kept to ~24% and concentrated in Weeks 3–4 after the audience already knows the café.

| Content Type   | Posts | Share | Examples                                      |
|----------------|-------|-------|-----------------------------------------------|
| Educational    | 5     | 29%   | Study tips, Pomodoro, Coffee 101, how cold brew is made |
| Engagement     | 4     | 24%   | Corner poll, playlist prompt, guessing teaser, photo contest |
| Informational  | 4     | 24%   | Café tour, meet the barista, work-from-café guide, reposts |
| Promotional    | 4     | 24%   | Cold brew launch, weekend offer, student combo, recap |

---

## Post Formats

- **Reels** — reach (café tour, launch, recap)
- **Carousels** — saves & shares (tips, guides)
- **Single images** — offers & prompts
- **Stories** — polls, Q&A, countdowns

## Engagement Strategy

Every week includes at least one post that invites action:
- Comment favourite corner
- Share a playlist
- Guess the new item
- Post a Quiet Cup moment

The Day 24 photo contest feeds the Day 27 repost carousel — followers become part of the content.

---

## Key Learnings

- Planning **weekly themes first** made daily topic selection much easier.
- Tying every post to a clear **objective** prevented a random list of ideas.
- Keeping promotion low early and building toward a launch created a coherent **story** across the month.
- A realistic frequency with **fixed posting times** is what makes consistency achievable.

---

## Conclusion

The calendar delivers **17 planned Instagram posts** across 30 days for The Quiet Cup, with clear topics, formats, captions, hashtags, a fixed publishing schedule, and a balanced content mix serving two objectives: brand awareness and the Signature Cold Brew launch.
